TRADEGUARD SAMPLE RISK POLICY
Document type: Synthetic demonstration policy
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
IMPORTANT: This is an illustrative policy for a software prototype. It is not a real bank policy or regulatory guidance.

1. Position limits
Each instrument has a configured position risk limit in the trading_positions.csv file.
Utilization is position market value divided by the configured limit.
Normal: utilization below 90%.
Warning: utilization from 90% through 100%, inclusive.
Critical: utilization above 100%.
A critical classification is a trigger for analyst review, not an automatic trading restriction.

2. Concentration monitoring
Sector concentration is sector market value divided by total portfolio market value.
For this prototype, flag a sector when its share is greater than 40%.
Concentration is a monitoring indicator and does not by itself prove a limit breach.

3. Value at Risk
The prototype reports one-day historical VaR at 95% confidence using the 5th percentile of simulated portfolio daily P&L.
VaR is a model estimate, not a maximum possible loss. Historical/synthetic VaR does not predict future losses.

4. Escalation and human review
Critical alerts should be prepared for review by a risk analyst.
The agent may summarize evidence and recommend review. It must not approve escalation or execute trades.
An authorized human reviewer records approve or reject with a comment.

5. Evidence and auditability
Risk amounts must come from deterministic calculation tools.
The language model may explain verified outputs but must not invent positions, limits, returns, or policy thresholds.
If evidence is unavailable, the system must state that limitation.
